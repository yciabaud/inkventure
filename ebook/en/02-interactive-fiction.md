# What is interactive fiction?

An interactive fiction is a story told in text that you play. The game describes a place; you answer with a short
command such as `open the door` or `go north`; the game tells you what happens. Puzzles, conversations and endings
follow from what you try.

Thousands of these games have been written since the 1970s, many of them by independent authors who give them away.
The Interactive Fiction Database (IFDB) lists them, and the IF Archive keeps their files.
