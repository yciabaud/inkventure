# Credits

**The games** belong to their authors, named on their cards. Most are free to play; each game's page on IFDB gives
its terms. If you enjoy one, tell its author: a review on IFDB is the best thanks.

**The catalogue** (game listings, descriptions, ratings and covers) comes from the Interactive Fiction Database,
ifdb.org, written by its players. **The game files** are downloaded from the IF Archive, ifarchive.org, hosted by
the Interactive Fiction Technology Foundation. Many thanks to the volunteers who keep both running.

**The app** runs the games on free interpreters: ZVM (ifvms.js) and GlkOte for Z-machine games, Quixe by Andrew
Plotkin for Glulx games, inkjs for ink stories; Twine stories run on their own story format. Its typefaces are
Literata and Source Sans 3, under the SIL Open Font License. Inkventure is free, open-source software under the MIT
licence. It has no advertising and no tracking.

**This book** exists in English and French, in EPUB and AZW3. Share it freely; it can be downloaded from a computer
at <{{host}}ebook/>.
