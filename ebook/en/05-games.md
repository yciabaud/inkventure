# Games to start with

First, a few stories chosen to begin with, then some of the best-loved games in the catalogue. Each card gives the
game's author, year and length, and a **Play now** link that opens it in Inkventure with the Wi-Fi on. The
**Start here** badge marks games that are kind to beginners: try one of those first.

The lengths are the median times reported by players on IFDB: you may well take longer, and that is fine.
