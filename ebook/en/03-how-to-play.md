# How to play

Type a command and confirm it. Commands are short sentences that start with a verb:

- `look` (or `l`) describes where you are again;
- `examine lamp` (or `x lamp`) looks closely at something;
- `take key`, `drop key`, `inventory` (or `i`);
- `open door`, `push button`, `talk to the keeper`.

To move, use the compass: `north`, `south`, `east`, `west` (or `n`, `s`, `e`, `w`), and `up`, `down`, `in`, `out`.

Inkventure saves your game after every turn. `undo` takes back your last move, and the reader's menu keeps save
slots for the moments you want to come back to.
