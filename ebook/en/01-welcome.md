# Welcome

[Open Inkventure]({{host}}){.open-app}

Tap the link above with the Wi-Fi on (the e-reader may ask you to confirm): the app opens in its web browser.

This little book is a door into interactive fiction: stories where you decide what happens next by telling the
game what to do. It explains how these games work, how to play them on the e-reader you are holding, and ends with
a shelf of games you can start from the page with one tap.

Inkventure is a free web app made for e-ink screens. There is no account to create and nothing to install: open
its address in your e-reader's web browser and pick a story. Thousands of games are waiting there, all free, all
playable a page at a time, like a book.

**In this book**

- What is interactive fiction? A few words on these games and where they come from.
- How to play: commands, the compass, saving.
- Inkventure on your e-reader: opening the app, turning pages, the reader's menu, where your games are kept.
- Games to start with: a selection, each with a link that opens it.
- Credits.

Already curious? Skip to *Games to start with* and tap **Play now** on the first card: the game explains itself as
you go, and this book will still be here when you want it.
