# Welcome

This book is a door into interactive fiction: stories where you decide what happens next by typing what you
want to do. You can read it anywhere, and play every game it presents on the e-reader you are holding, for free.

Inkventure is a free web app made for e-ink screens. It needs no account and no installation: open its address in
your e-reader's web browser and pick a story.

_This edition is a draft: the full text comes with the first release._
