# Inkventure on your e-reader

1. Turn on the Wi-Fi.
2. Open the web browser (on some e-readers it is in the menu, sometimes under "experimental").
3. Go to <{{host}}>.

The links in this book open the game directly: follow one, confirm that you want to open the web page, and the
story starts. On a device without a browser, scan the card's QR code with a phone instead.

Your progress is kept in the e-reader's browser, on the device itself: nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing the
browser's data erases it.
