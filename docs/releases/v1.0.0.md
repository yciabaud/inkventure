The first public release of Inkventure: play interactive fiction from IFDB in your e-reader's web browser, with
nothing to install and no account.

- **Four formats:** Z-machine, Glulx, ink and Twine (experimental), downloaded from the IF Archive and the other hosts
  listed on IFDB.
- **Home:** a hand-picked Featured shelf, *My adventures* and a *Continue* button that reopens the last game.
- **Library:** text search, filters (genre, language, format, rating, play time, illustrated, "Start here") and
  sorting; a page for each game with its cover, blurb and details.
- **Reader for e-ink:** text in pages turned by taps, no scrolling and no animations; text settings (typeface, size,
  margins).
- **Parser games without much typing:** verb chips, chips for the objects the parser asks about and for the commands
  the text names in capitals, yes/no and numbered answers, single-key prompts, menus and quote boxes drawn in the
  status window, the full status line.
- **Choice-based games:** ink choices as large buttons; Twine stories in a sandboxed frame, with their animations
  jumped to the end and their colours made readable in grey.
- **Pictures** in Glulx games, in grey, and an *Illustrated* filter.
- **Saves:** autosave and resume, five save slots, undo.
- **Offline:** the app opens without Wi-Fi once loaded, and games marked *Keep offline* play without it.
- **English and French** interface, and a **free ebook** in both languages whose game cards open the games in the
  app.
- **Private:** no account, no tracking, no cookies; progress stays in the browser.

Thanks to IFDB, the IF Archive and every author whose game is in the catalogue.

### Known issues

- Twine support is experimental: stories that rely on heavy scripting or styling may not play fully.
- Opening the app from the ebook's link with Wi-Fi off has not been checked on a device yet.
