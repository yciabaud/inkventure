## User story

As a player of a game that does something on a clock (text that appears by itself, a countdown, an animation), I want
that to happen on my e-reader, so that the game plays as its author meant, and never waits forever.

## Background

The [story-file scan of 2026-10-04](../reports/story-file-scan-2026-10-04.md) (S7.4) found **116 games** that use time:
85 Z-machine games with timed input (`read_char` / `aread` with a time and a routine, e.g. Shrapnel, Fail-Safe, Make It
Good) and 31 Glulx games that request timer events (e.g. Best of Three, NewGrant, Los alegres hombres de Sherwood,
which start a 10–40 ms timer at their intro, for animations). The reader never sends a timer: the GlkOte bridge
declares no optional feature (`support: []`), and ZVM's timed input is given no time. Whatever a game does on a clock
does not happen. Most games treat time as optional, but some wait for it.

On e-ink, a fast timer would redraw the screen many times a second: it must be slowed down.

## Scope

- **Glulx:** the bridge declares `timer` support and sends timer events, at the interval the game asks for but **never
  more often than once a second**; no timer while the reader is not on the game (another screen, the app hidden).
- **Z-machine:** timed input calls its routine on time (ZVM / glkote-term), with the same floor; check what ZVM does
  with the time today.
- **Redraws:** text or status rows that arrive on a timer are shown without moving the reader off the page being read
  (only on the last page, as a turn's text is).
- Choose and record what a game that animates (redraws every tick) looks like on the Kindle; a game may need its timer
  slowed further, or ignored, if it hurts the e-ink screen.
- SPEC §4 and §3.6 updated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fixture with a timer (Z-machine timed `read_char`, Glulx timer): text appears after its delay, without a key.
- [ ] Shrapnel, Fail-Safe (Z-machine) and Best of Three (Glulx): their timed events happen (survey replay or by hand).
- [ ] No timer events faster than once a second; none while the reader is not on the game.
- [ ] Checked on the PR preview and on the Kindle (screen refreshes acceptable), or the Notes say why not.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- The bridge: `timer` declared, timer events sent at the requested interval with the 1 s floor, stopped on request (0).
- ZVM: timed `read_char` calls its routine.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- Fixture games (extend `lamp.inf`, Z-machine and Glulx, rebuilt and committed): a command whose text appears after a
  delay, with a fake clock (`page.clock`).

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, size budgets, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Sound (silent by design).
- Graphics windows and their animations.
