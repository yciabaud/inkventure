## User story

As the product owner, I want to know whether a Decker deck can be played on the Kindle with a patched runtime, so
that Decker games can be decided on facts, before any catalogue or engine work.

## Background

[post-v1-ideas.md](../post-v1-ideas.md) ranks **Decker** (John Earnest's HyperCard-like tool, MIT) as the most
promising new format: a pure-JS runtime (`js/lil.js` and `js/decker.js`, about 127 KB gz), a documented text format,
1-bit cards of 512 × 342 that fit the Kindle's 636 × 740 viewport at 1:1, and decks listed on IFDB as web games. Its
blockers on the Kindle are known only from reading the code: `sync()` recomputes every pixel on every
`requestAnimationFrame`, transitions, `sleep` and animated patterns assume 60 fps, and input uses mouse and touch
events.

## Scope

- A standalone page `public/probe/decker/` (no build dependency, like the device and offline probes), with a pinned
  web-decker release (record version and licence) and **one real deck** whose licence allows it (record the deck, its
  author and its licence; one written by us if none does).
- **Patch the runtime**, as small patches applied by a script and kept in the repo:
  - redraw only when the card or a widget changed (dirty flag), never on an idle frame;
  - transitions jump to their end; animated patterns are frozen; `sleep` and `sys.frame` keep working at a low rate;
  - the editor and its menus are hidden or left out, if that trims the runtime.
- **Measure on the Kindle**, shown on the page and as QR codes (like the probes): runtime parse and start time, first
  card drawn, a card change, a button script, a field typed into with the on-screen keyboard; CPU idle on a static
  card (no redraw in 10 s); whether `new ImageData(w, h)`, `{ passive: false }` listeners and touch events work.
- **Size:** the runtime's size gz, as patched, against the 100 KiB lazy-chunk budget (`size-budget.json`), and what
  would be needed to fit (trimming, or a budget discussion with the owner).
- A report in `docs/device-reports/` and a recommendation in `post-v1-ideas.md` (go: a story for a Decker engine and a
  curated shelf; or no-go, and why). SPEC §13 updated with the answer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The probe page plays the deck in Chromium with the patches: cards change on taps, and no frame is drawn while
      nothing changes.
- [ ] A Kindle report recorded in `docs/device-reports/` with the measures above.
- [ ] A go / no-go recommendation in `post-v1-ideas.md`, and SPEC §13 updated.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- The patch script applies to the pinned release and fails loudly if the upstream code it patches moved.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- The probe page loads the deck, a tap on a button changes the card, and no redraw happens on an idle card over a few
  seconds (counted by the patched `sync`).

Plus the global gates: lint, size budgets (the probe is outside the app's budgets but its size is reported), CI green.
`check:es5` covers `dist/probe/**`, but Decker is ES2017 (which the Kindle's engine runs, SPEC §2.2): leave
`dist/probe/decker/` out of it and record why.

## Out of scope

- A Decker engine in the reader, catalogue entries or an itch.io scraper (after the go / no-go).
- Bitsy, DAAD and the other formats of `post-v1-ideas.md`.
- Sound.
