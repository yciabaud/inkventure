## User story

As a player, I want text laid out as the author wrote it, so that centred titles, separators and status details
look like they do in other interpreters.

## Background

Found on 2026-10-01 (featured games, production vs Parchment). None of these blocks play.

1. **Leading spaces are collapsed.** 9:05 prints `  -----` around its title. Anchorhead centres
   "A N C H O R H E A D" with spaces. The text is intact in the DOM (`reader__run`), but the block's `white-space`
   collapses the spaces. Parchment keeps them.
2. **9:05's status line** shows "Moves: 0" where Parchment shows "Time: 9:05 am". Both run ZVM, so the difference
   probably comes from the header flags we set (v5 Flags 1 bit 1, "pictures available"). The Inform library may
   test this bit to choose between the time and the score. This is not verified.
3. **Twine edges**: SugarCube's UI-bar toggle is cut at the left edge, and a few glyphs overflow at the right edge
   (Detritus, The Den).

## Scope

- Spaces: keep leading spaces and runs of spaces in the reader's text blocks, like Parchment (`white-space`
  `pre-wrap` or an equivalent), with wrapping kept. Check the paginator: it measures the DOM, but its word-start
  splitting must not break on preserved spaces. Check games that put two spaces after a full stop (Violet): the
  text should still look fine, or the Notes record the choice.
- 9:05: find why the status differs (compare our ZVM header and Glk gestalt answers with Parchment's). Fix it if
  ours is wrong. Otherwise record why we differ.
- Twine edges: the UI-bar toggle is fully visible and ≥ 48 px, and nothing overflows the frame horizontally
  (no horizontal scroll), in SugarCube and Harlowe.
- SPEC updated where behaviour changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 9:05 shows `  -----` indented, and Anchorhead's title is centred as in Parchment.
- [ ] 9:05 shows "Time: 9:05 am", or the Notes explain the difference.
- [ ] Detritus and The Den: the UI-bar toggle is whole and there is no overflow on the right.
- [ ] Pagination of a long game text (Counterfeit Monkey's intro, Lost Pig) is unchanged or better (no cut words).

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Paginator with preserved spaces: leading spaces, double spaces, a long line of spaces that must wrap.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- The Z-machine fixture prints an indented line, and the e2e checks its rendered left offset is larger than a
  plain line's.
- A SugarCube fixture at the e-reader size: the UI-bar toggle is inside the frame and `scrollWidth <= clientWidth`.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, size budgets, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Monospace / fixed-pitch styles (`preformatted` runs keep the reader's font).
