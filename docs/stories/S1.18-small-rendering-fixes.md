## User story

As a player, I want text laid out as the author wrote it, so that centred titles, separators and status details
look like they do in other interpreters.

## Background

Found on 2026-10-01 (featured games, production vs Parchment). None of these blocks play.

1. **Leading spaces are collapsed.** 9:05 prints `  -----` around its title. Anchorhead centres
   "A N C H O R H E A D" with spaces. The text is intact in the DOM (`reader__run`), but the block's `white-space`
   collapses the spaces. Parchment keeps them.
2. **9:05's status line** shows "Moves: 0" where Parchment shows "Time: 9:05 am". Both run ZVM, so the difference
   probably comes from the header flags we set (v5 Flags 1 bit 1, "pictures available"). The Inform library may
   test this bit to choose between the time and the score. This is not verified.
3. **Twine edges**: SugarCube's UI-bar toggle is cut at the left edge, and a few glyphs overflow at the right edge
   (Detritus, The Den).
4. **"Back to the present ›"** (added by the owner on 2026-10-03): the button under the text on any page but the last
   ("Retour au présent ›" in French) is confusing. It shows when the player reads earlier pages, and also right after
   a reply longer than a page, where it only means "the rest is further on".

## Scope

- Spaces: keep leading spaces and runs of spaces in the reader's text blocks, like Parchment (`white-space`
  `pre-wrap` or an equivalent), with wrapping kept. Check the paginator: it measures the DOM, but its word-start
  splitting must not break on preserved spaces. Check games that put two spaces after a full stop (Violet): the
  text should still look fine, or the Notes record the choice.
- 9:05: find why the status differs (compare our ZVM header and Glk gestalt answers with Parchment's). Fix it if
  ours is wrong. Otherwise record why we differ.
- Twine edges: the UI-bar toggle is fully visible and ≥ 48 px, and nothing overflows the frame horizontally
  (no horizontal scroll), in SugarCube and Harlowe.
- The button becomes **"Last page ›"** / **"Dernière page ›"**: it says what it does (it opens the last page, where
  the command bar is), in both cases.
- SPEC updated where behaviour changes.

## Notes & decisions

- **"Last page ›" / "Dernière page ›"** (key `reader.lastPage`, was `reader.backToPresent`): the label says what the
  button does. The ebook's "On your e-reader" chapter (EN, FR) names it too and is updated.
- **Spaces:** `white-space: pre-wrap` on the reader's text blocks (game view, Transcript view, Ink). Lines still wrap;
  a page still opens on a word, never on spaces. Checked with the real files at the Kindle viewport: 9:05's `  -----`
  lines are indented; Anchorhead's spaced-out title and its `[Press 'R' …]` line
  keep their offsets; Violet's two spaces after a full stop show as a slightly wider gap, as in Parchment (kept: it
  is how the author typed it).
- **9:05's clock — found:** in versions 4 and later, ZVM 1.1.6 rewrites Flags 1 and clears bit 1, which the Inform
  library reads at startup (`sys_statusline_flag`) to show the time instead of the score. Upstream ZVM fixed it after
  1.1.6 ("Preserve bit 1 … it is used by Inform", ifvms.js master); a build-time patch (`scripts/build/vendor-patches.ts`)
  does the same here. Real 9:05 now shows "Time: 9:05 am". In unit tests ZVM's modules are loaded by Node's `require`
  and are not patched, so this is checked in e2e with a new fixture, `tests/fixtures/zmachine/clock.inf` (a time game
  that also prints an indented line), served as the sample catalogue's Lamp game.
- **Twine edges — found:** at e-reader widths SugarCube stows its UI bar and leaves a 2em strip for the toggle, sized
  for its own font; with the reader's larger text the toggle was wider (40–45 px), started at x = −7 to −18 px and was
  under 48 px. The e-ink stylesheet now gives the toggle 48 × 48 px at least, leaves a 56 px strip
  (`left: calc(56px - 17.5em)`) and moves the story's left margin to 64 px. Detritus and The Den: the toggle is at
  x = 6–8 px, 48 × 48. The overflow on the right reported on 2026-10-01 no longer reproduces (`scrollWidth` equals
  `clientWidth` on both; it was probably the colours or the margins fixed in S1.13 / S1.14); the e2e checks it.
- **Pagination, a pre-existing bug fixed:** a line that starts inside a hyphenated word ("verb-noun-" /
  "preposition-noun.") was not seen as a line by the measurement, which looked for word starts only, so the
  paginator could put one line too many on a page and the last line was cut at the bottom (found on Violet at the
  largest font, Transcript view; the same with or without the spaces change). Line starts are now also looked for
  after a hyphen (`lineStartCandidates`, `src/reader/measure.ts`); a page can then end after "verb-noun-". Checked on
  Lost Pig and Violet at the largest font and the small e-reader size: every split paragraph continues exactly on the
  next page, on a word, and no page overflows. Counterfeit Monkey (Glulx) was too slow to drive in the container, so
  it was not part of that check.
- **Tests:** the paginator itself works on line metrics and does not see spaces, so the "paginator with preserved
  spaces" unit test is on `lineStartCandidates` (leading spaces, double spaces, a long run of spaces, hyphens). E2E in
  `tests/e2e/rendering.spec.ts`: the time game's status line, an indented line's left offset, and the SugarCube
  fixture's stowed toggle at 600 px (both fail without their fix).
