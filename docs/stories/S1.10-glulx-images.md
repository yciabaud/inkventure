## User story

As a player of an illustrated Inform 7 game, I want to see its pictures in the text so that I do not miss part of the
story.

## Scope

- Split from S1.7. Glk images drawn in the main (buffer) window (`glk_image_draw`, from the Blorb's `Pict` chunks)
  become an image block in the transcript: the bridge passes the image number and size, the engine resolves its data.
- Pagination: an image is sized from its known width and height (scaled to the page width, capped to a page height)
  before it loads, so pages never reflow.
- Shown in grayscale (CSS filter), with its alt text when the Blorb has one.
- Saved transcript tail keeps a reference (image number), not the image data; resolved again after a reload.
- A Glulx fixture with one small PNG in a `.gblorb`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The fixture's picture shows on its page, in grayscale, and is still there after a reload.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Bridge: image specials → image blocks; paginator: an image block's height from its size.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- Play the illustrated fixture: the picture is visible, pages do not overflow, reload keeps it.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Graphics windows (canvas), sound.

## Notes & decisions

- **Engine → reader:** a new `image` `OutputBlock` (`ImageRef`: Blorb picture number, width, height, alt text from
  `RDes`). The bridge reads Quixe's `{special: 'image'}` items in buffer window lines; a picture always gets a line of
  its own (the text before and after it on the same Glk line becomes separate paragraphs, and appended text never joins
  a picture). All alignments (`inlineup`, `marginleft`…) are shown the same way: centred, on their own line. The data
  comes from the new optional `Engine.imageUrl(image)` (Quixe's Blorb decoder builds a `data:` URL once per picture
  and caches it); the transcript and the reader only hold the number.
- **Pagination:** `imageBox()` (paginator) scales a picture down to the text column width and to the room on the last
  page (page height minus what the command bar covers), keeping its proportions; so a picture always fits on any page
  and is never split. The measuring layer and the page render the same box (explicit px size), so measuring never waits
  for the picture to load. The metrics cache is also keyed by that height cap when a picture is among the reused
  blocks. Because the last page holds less text, a picture that would fit on a full page may move to the next one,
  like any text there (seen on the fixture at 600 × 800).
- **Grayscale:** `filter: grayscale(100%)` on `.reader__image` (autoprefixer adds `-webkit-filter` for old WebKit). A
  picture without data (an engine that has none) shows its alt text in a bordered box of its size.
- **Saves:** a saved transcript paragraph is now `TextRun[] | ImageRef` (old saves are plain arrays, still read). The
  tail counts a picture as one block and stores no image data; after a reload the engine resolves it again.
- **Fixture:** `tests/fixtures/glulx/picture.inf` (raw Glk, MIT) packed with a 600 × 400 colour PNG and its alt text
  into `picture.gblorb` (6 KB) by `scripts/fixtures/build-blorb.ts` (draws the PNG with `node:zlib`, writes the Blorb),
  run by `build-glulx.sh`. Served at `#/play/fixture-glulx-picture` (fixtures can now have their own title).
- **Size:** Kindle first load 192.3 KiB gz (+0.8 KiB: bridge, paginator and reader code), budget 200 KiB.
- **WebKit:** not installable in the dev sandbox; the e2e test runs on WebKit in CI only.
- Follow-ups (not in scope): graphics windows (canvas), a picture's hyperlink, the Blorb cover (`Fspc`) on the game
  page.
