## User story

As a player of a parser game on an e-reader, I want to tap an exit in the compass the game draws in its status
window, so that I can move without looking for the direction in the command bar.

## Background

Reported by the owner on 2026-10-02, on the Kindle, after [S1.15](S1.15-status-line-rows.md). Since S1.15, the top
zone shows every row of the status window as text, so Bronze's compass of exits shows on the right:

```
Entrance Hall                 N
Main Castle               W . E
Rooms searched: 2/55       SW S
```

The "." in the middle stands for the player. Tapping "N" does what any tap on the top zone does: it opens the
reader menu (⋯). The player expects to go north. Parchment shows the same compass and does not make it tappable
either, so this goes beyond what the original interpreter does.

The directions are already one tap away in the command bar (S1.4: N, S, E, W, Up, Down and a dialog with all twelve),
but they list every direction, not the exits the game shows.

## Scope

- **Recognise a compass** in the right parts of the status rows (`statusRows`, S1.15): every token is a direction
  abbreviation of the game's language (`N`, `NE`, `E`, `SE`, `S`, `SW`, `W`, `NW`, `U` / `UP`, `D` / `DOWN`, `IN`,
  `OUT`; in French also `O`, `NO`, `SO`, `HAUT`, `BAS`, `DEDANS`, `DEHORS`; reuse the direction table of the verb
  chips) or a separator (`.`, `-`, `|`). Choose and record the rule against false positives: e.g. at least two
  directions across the rows, or a `.` among them. A score, a move counter, a time or a region name is not a compass.
- **Tap targets of at least 48 px** (SPEC §2.3). The zone's rows are about one UI line tall, so choose and record one
  of the following:
  - (a) Each exit is its own target, and the zone grows to fit them (limit how much: the text area shrinks with it).
  - (b) **Recommended:** a tap anywhere on the compass opens the directions dialog of the command bar, with the exits
    the compass shows first (or the only ones enabled). It is one more tap, but every target stays ≥ 48 px and the top
    zone keeps its height.
- A direction is sent as the command bar's direction chip would send it (game language), only while the game waits
  for a line. While it waits for a key (a menu), the compass is plain text and the zone opens the menu as before.
- The rest of the top zone (location, other rows, ⋯) still opens the reader menu.
- SPEC §3.6 (Status line) updated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Bronze: tapping the compass leads to going north (directly, or through the dialog with option b), and the
      game answers with the next room; the compass then shows the new room's exits.
- [ ] One-row status lines (Lost Pig, Counterfeit Monkey, The Lamp at Saltmere outside the shed) and score / moves
      counters are not taken for a compass: the zone behaves as before.
- [ ] Every target is at least 48 px; the text area is not cut and the pages are laid out again if the zone's height
      changes (S1.15).
- [ ] Checked on the PR preview and on the Kindle.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Compass detection: Bronze's rows (`N` / `W . E` / `SW S`), the Z-machine fixture's shed rows (`W  OUT`), a French
  compass, and negatives: `Score: 0  Moves: 2`, `Turns: 12`, a time (`9:05`), a region name, a single letter that is
  part of a word.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- In the Z-machine fixture's shed (three status rows, `W  OUT` on the right of the second one), tap the compass and
  leave the shed (`out`); the status line goes back to one row. Outside the shed, a tap on the status line still opens
  the reader menu.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, size budgets, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Drawing the upper window as a grid (fixed columns, reverse video), as in S1.15.
- Guessing exits from the room description, or showing only the room's exits in the command bar's direction row.
- Games that draw a compass in a graphics window or as a picture.
