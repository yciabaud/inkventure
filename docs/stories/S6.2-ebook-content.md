## User story

As a curious reader, I want a short, friendly guide to interactive fiction so that I know how to play before opening the app.

## Scope

- Chapters: What is IF · How to play (commands, compass, verbs, saving) · Using Inkventure on your e-reader (Wi-Fi, browser, where saves are kept) · Featured games · Credits.
- EN and FR versions; tone reviewed by the owner.
- Screenshots of the app in grayscale.
