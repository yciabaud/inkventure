## User story

As a returning player, I want to resume my last game in one tap and see all my adventures so that I get back into the story quickly.

## Scope

- Continue hero from latest progress (cover, title, turn, relative date).
- My adventures shelf: grid/list toggle, sort recent/title, remove from home (optionally delete saves).
- Games auto-added to My adventures when first played.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] After playing a game, Home shows it in Continue and My adventures; removing works and respects the saves choice.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Sorting, relative date formatting (EN/FR), removal with/without saves.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- Play fixture game, go Home, Continue resumes at same turn; switch grid/list persists.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Collections/folders.

## Notes & decisions

- **Code**: `src/storage/home.ts` (`myAdventures` with the progress of each game, `sortAdventures`, `continueGame`,
  `removeFromHome` with `deleteSaves`), `HomeScreen.tsx` (hero, My adventures, Featured), compact `Pager`, prefs
  `homeView` / `homeSort`. `GameReader` adds the game to My adventures on its first turn (title, author, cover flag
  from the catalogue detail; the test adventure too).
- **Continue hero**: the game of My adventures played last (a game removed from Home is not offered), with its
  cover, title, "Turn 142 · last played 2 days ago" (`formatRelativeDate`, EN/FR) and a Continue button to
  `#/play/<tuid>`, which resumes from the autosave. Hidden until a game is started.
- **Layout**: the hero, My adventures and Featured share the screen without scrolling (checked at 636×740 and
  360×640): each shelf has one header row (title, tools, "‹ 2 / 5 ›"), and cards show only their title when both
  shelves are there (the Featured pitches remain on a first launch). On a phone the header wraps.
- **Tools**: two buttons naming what they switch to ("List" / "Grid", "A–Z" / "Recent"), with explicit accessible
  names ("List view", "Sort by title"). "Recent" = last played, or added for a game never played.
- **Remove**: "⋯" per game (a 48 px button on the cover corner, or at the end of a list row) → menu with the game's
  page and Remove from Home → confirmation with "Also delete its saves and progress" (off by default). The cached
  story file is kept either way (it is evicted when room is needed).
- **Tests**: unit `home.test.ts` (sorting, Continue, removal with and without saves, cover flag, progress);
  relative dates were already covered (S1.5). E2E `home-adventures.spec.ts`: play the test adventure, Home shows it
  in Continue and My adventures, Continue resumes at the same turn; grid/list and sort survive a reload; removal
  keeps or deletes the saves. `home.spec.ts` uses the compact pager's "Next".
- **Follow-ups**: long-press as a shortcut to the "⋯" menu (SPEC mentions it; the button is enough on e-ink).
