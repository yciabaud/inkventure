## User story

As a returning player, I want to resume my last game in one tap and see all my adventures so that I get back into the story quickly.

## Scope

- Continue hero from latest progress (cover, title, turn, relative date).
- My adventures shelf: grid/list toggle, sort recent/title, remove from home (optionally delete saves).
- Games auto-added to My adventures when first played.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After playing a game, Home shows it in Continue and My adventures; removing works and respects the saves choice.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Sorting, relative date formatting (EN/FR), removal with/without saves.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- Play fixture game, go Home, Continue resumes at same turn; switch grid/list persists.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, CI green.
