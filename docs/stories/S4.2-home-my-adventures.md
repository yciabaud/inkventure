## User story

As a returning player, I want to resume my last game in one tap and see all my adventures so that I get back into the story quickly.

## Scope

- Continue hero from latest progress (cover, title, turn, relative date).
- My adventures shelf: grid/list toggle, sort recent/title, remove from home (optionally delete saves).
  _Revised with the owner: covers only, last played first (see Notes)._
- Games auto-added to My adventures when first played.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] After playing a game, Home shows it in Continue and My adventures; removing works and respects the saves choice.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Sorting, relative date formatting (EN/FR), removal with/without saves.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- Play fixture game, go Home, Continue resumes at same turn; switch grid/list persists (_dropped with the list view_).

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Collections/folders.

## Notes & decisions

- **Code**: `src/storage/home.ts` (`myAdventures` with the progress of each game, `sortAdventures`, `continueGame`,
  `removeFromHome` with `deleteSaves`), `HomeScreen.tsx` (hero, My adventures, Featured), compact `Pager`. `GameReader` adds the game to My adventures on its first turn (title, author, cover flag
  from the catalogue detail; the test adventure too).
- **Continue hero**: the game of My adventures played last (a game removed from Home is not offered), with its
  cover, title, "Turn 142 · last played 2 days ago" (`formatRelativeDate`, EN/FR) and a Continue button to
  `#/play/<tuid>`, which resumes from the autosave. Hidden until a game is started.
- **Layout**: no scrolling (checked at 636×740 and 360×640): each shelf has one header row (title or tabs, then
  "‹ 2 / 5 ›").
- **No list view nor sort** (owner's review of the preview: "too much here"): the scope's grid/list toggle and
  recent/title sort were built, then removed; My adventures is a grid of covers, last played (or added) first. The
  header (title, ‹ ›) stays on one line on a phone too.
- **One shelf at a time** (owner's review: covers too small with both shelves stacked): with adventures and
  featured games, tabs "My adventures | Featured" (`?shelf=featured`, replaced in the history, not pushed) show one
  shelf under the hero; covers up to 250 px tall (IFDB's largest thumbnail). On narrow screens the compact pager
  hides its page number so the tabs and ‹ › keep one line.
- **Game menu**: a "⋮" over the bottom right corner of each cover (black dots with a white halo, no frame, so the
  cover stays visible; a 48 px target), like
  the Kindle library, opens a dialog with the game (cover, title, author, progress) and its actions: Continue (or
  Play), Game page, Remove from Home → confirmation with "Also delete its saves and progress" (off by default). The
  cached story file is kept either way (it is evicted when room is needed).
- **Tests**: unit `home.test.ts` (sorting, Continue, removal with and without saves, cover flag, progress);
  relative dates were already covered (S1.5). E2E `home-adventures.spec.ts`: play the test adventure, Home shows it
  in Continue and My adventures, Continue resumes at the same turn; last played first and the ⋮ menu (game,
  progress, Continue); the Featured tab; removal keeps or deletes the saves. `home.spec.ts` uses the compact pager's "Next".
- **Follow-ups**: long-press as a shortcut to the "⋮" menu (SPEC mentions it; the button is enough on e-ink).
