## User story

As a player of a parser game, I want the rows the game draws in its upper window in bold or reverse video to stand
out in the reader, so that I can see a menu's selected entry or a title as the game meant it.

## Background

The [rendering survey of 2026-10-05](../reports/rendering-survey-2026-10-05.md) (S7.3, "Styles in the upper window")
finds rows of the upper window in different styles that the reader shows all alike: City of Secrets' HELP (normal and
subheader), Hunter, in Darkness and The Act of Misdirection at their intro, Slouching Towards Bedlam's HELP. S1.22
already shows a menu entry marked "> " in bold, but a menu that marks its selection only with reverse video (as many
Inform menus do) shows no selection at all. The status rows (`statusRows`, S1.15) and the boxes (S1.23) keep the text
of each row and drop its style.

## Scope

- Keep the style of each run in the upper-window rows the reader shows (status rows, S1.22 menu screens, S1.23
  boxes), not only its text.
- **Mapping for e-ink** (no colour): `header`, `subheader` and `alert` in bold, `emphasized` in italics, as in the
  text area. **Reverse video** (Z-machine `set_text_style reverse`, Glk `reverse` style hint or the status window's
  default style) is the selection or the status bar itself: choose and record how it shows, e.g. a row whose only
  reverse run is the whole row of a menu is the selected entry (bold, with a "›" before it), and the top zone ignores
  the reverse video of the status bar as today.
- No change to the top zone's layout or height; the "> " rule of S1.22 stays.
- SPEC §3.6 (Status line, menus, boxes) updated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In the Z-machine fixture's GUIDE menu, drawn again with the selection in reverse video and no "> ", the selected
      entry is the one shown as selected, and it moves with N and P.
- [ ] A title row in bold or subheader shows in bold in the top zone and in a menu screen.
- [ ] The four games named above show their styled rows as above; the rest of the survey's games are unchanged (run
      `npm run survey:rendering` before and after, and compare).
- [ ] Checked on the PR preview and on the Kindle.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Status rows and menu rows keep their runs' styles; the reverse-video selection rule (a selected row, a reversed
  status bar that is not a selection, a row with reverse video on one word only).

**E2E (Playwright)**

- The fixture's GUIDE menu (Z-machine) with a reverse-video selection: the selected entry is marked and follows the
  keys.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, size budgets, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Colours (ignored on e-ink, SPEC §1.2).
- Drawing the upper window as a grid with fixed columns.
- Fixed-width lines that wrap ([S1.28](S1.28-wide-fixed-width-text.md)).
