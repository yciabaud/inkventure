## User story

As a player of a parser game that draws a map, a table or a menu in fixed-width text, I want its lines to keep their
columns on my e-reader, so that I can read what the game laid out.

## Background

The [rendering survey of 2026-10-05](../reports/rendering-survey-2026-10-05.md) (S7.3, "Wide fixed-width text") finds
fixed-width lines laid out in columns longer than about 40 characters, which wrap on a 600 px screen: Christminster's
HELP settings (60 characters), Gourmet, Make It Good and The Act of Misdirection at their intro. The
[story-file scan](../reports/story-file-scan-2026-10-04.md) counts **63 games** that switch to a fixed-width font in
their own code. A preformatted run is shown with `white-space: pre-wrap` (`.reader__run--preformatted`), so a wide line
breaks in the middle of a column and the table no longer reads.

## Scope

- **Fit a fixed-width block to the text area's width:** a paragraph whose runs are all preformatted (or a group of
  such paragraphs, as one block) is shown without wrapping, with a font size reduced so that its longest line fits.
  Choose and record a floor (e.g. 11 px: under it, the block wraps as today, so nothing is cut or scrolled sideways).
- The block keeps the reader's line height rules and is paginated like any paragraph (a block taller than a page is
  split between lines, never inside one).
- Measured once per layout (font size, margins, orientation from the Aa menu, S1.2): no measurement per page turn.
- Mixed paragraphs (some fixed-width words in proportional text) are unchanged.
- SPEC §3.6 updated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fixture table of 60 columns shows on one line per row at the default reader settings on a 600 px wide
      viewport, and on two lines per row only past the floor (largest font size in the Aa menu).
- [ ] Christminster's HELP settings and Make It Good's intro keep their columns (survey replay, or by hand).
- [ ] Page turns stay under the 300 ms target on the Kindle (SPEC §4.5) on a page with such a block.
- [ ] Checked on the PR preview and on the Kindle.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- The font size chosen for a block: a narrow block keeps the reader's size, a wide one gets the size that fits, one
  past the floor keeps the floor and wraps.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- A new fixture command (TABLE in `lamp.inf`, Z-machine) printing a 60-column table in fixed-width font: at 600 px
  wide, every row is one line box; at the largest font size, the rows wrap and nothing overflows the text area.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, size budgets, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Fixed-width rows of the upper window (they are shown as text by S1.15, S1.22 and S1.23).
- Z-machine font 3 (character graphics), drawn as letters.
- Horizontal scrolling (SPEC §2.3: pages instead of scrolling).
