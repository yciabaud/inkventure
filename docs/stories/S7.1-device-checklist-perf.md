## User story

As the maintainer, I want a repeatable device test and automatic budgets so that releases work on real e-readers.

## Notes & decisions

- **Timings** (beyond the scope's wording, needed to measure SPEC §10's targets on the device): the *Turn times*
  setting became **Timings** (same `turnTimes` preference, and `?perf=1`). It also shows, in a small line at the top
  right that taps go through, the time to Home ready, to the Library's first results and of a page turn, and logs
  them to the console (`src/app/perf.ts`, `src/ui/PerfLine.tsx`). A screen counts from the page's start when it is the
  first one shown (a cold start), else from the address change; a page turn from the tap until the new page is
  painted.
- **Checklist:** `docs/device-checklist.md`, with real games of the catalogue (9:05 for Z-machine, Three-Card Trick and
  Brain Guzzlers from Beyond! for Glulx, the bundled Ink fixture since the catalogue has no Ink game, the Twine
  fixture or Jeangille) and a results table. **Release notes:** `docs/release-notes-template.md`.
- **First Kindle run** (2026-10-01, [report](../device-reports/2026-10-01-kindle-checklist.md)): Home ready 1.9 s,
  Library first results 2.2 s, page turns 109–191 ms, all well within SPEC §10's targets; save / restore / undo /
  resume, Ink and Twine fine. The owner saw no change of the timings line while playing: game turns were only in the
  status line, so they now also go to the timings line (`Turn played in … ms`). Some games did not work: to be named.
- **Turn times** (measured after the fix): 9:05 (Z-machine) 42–178 ms, Three-Card Trick (Glulx) 5–889 ms, within the
  1 s and 3 s targets.
- **Budgets confirmed, unchanged:** every target is met with margin (Home 1.9 s of 3 s, Library 2.2 s of 4 s, page
  turns ≤ 191 ms of 300 ms). The *First load* budget keeps measuring the legacy bundle with `.woff` (195.5 of 200 KiB):
  the Kindle loads the lighter modern bundle and `woff2` (probe, S0.3), so the budget is the worst case of older
  e-readers and keeps the margin there.
- **Follow-ups:** "some games do not work" (owner, Kindle run): the games are to be named, then investigated in their
  own story. Ink games: none in the catalogue, because they are published as web exports → [S2.7](S2.7-ink-web-exports.md).
