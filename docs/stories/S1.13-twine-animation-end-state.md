## User story

As a player of a Twine story, I want to see text that the author fades in, so that the story does not stay blank
on e-ink.

## Background

Found on 2026-10-01 (featured games, production, Kindle viewport). **Will Not Let Me Go** (`67r2qc21m5nzexv1`,
Harlowe) stays **blank**. The original shows a picture and "Funerals are a kind of spectator sport…" within a
second.

- The story's stylesheet has `.fadeIn{opacity:0; animation:fadeIn .8s forwards}` and puts `fadeIn` on `tw-story`.
- The e-ink stylesheet we inject sets `*,*::before,*::after{animation:none!important;transition:none!important}`
  (`src/engines/twine/twineHtml.ts`). With no animation, `tw-story` keeps `opacity:0`, so the whole story is
  invisible. The text is in the DOM (checked by opening the frame's `srcdoc` outside the sandbox: `tw-story`
  computed opacity `0`).
- The same story has `.fadeInOut`, `.pulseWithDelay` and others that start at `opacity:0` (a "[what…]" link
  among them).

## Scope

- Replace "no animation" with "zero-length animation". The animation still runs, but at once and only once, so the
  end state (`forwards` / `both` fill) applies with no movement on screen:
  `animation-duration:0s!important; animation-delay:0s!important; animation-iteration-count:1!important`.
  For transitions, `transition-duration:0s!important; transition-delay:0s!important` instead of
  `transition:none`.
- Check that no e-ink rule depended on `animation:none`: SugarCube's passage fade, Harlowe's `t8n` transitions,
  `(live:)` / timed text. Record what changed.
- A looping animation now freezes on its last frame. Check Will Not Let Me Go's pulsing "[what…]" link and record
  how it looks. If it ends invisible (last keyframe `opacity:0`), choose a fix (e.g. force `opacity:1` on links)
  and record it.
- SPEC §4.3 updated ("no animations or transitions": they jump to their end state).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Will Not Let Me Go shows its first passage (picture + text) and its links can be followed, on a desktop browser
      and on the Kindle. _Desktop: checked on the PR preview (Chrome, Kindle viewport). Kindle: checked by the owner (about 2 s to load)._
- [x] Birdland, The Den, Detritus and the Twine fixtures look the same as before.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- The injected stylesheet contains the zero-duration rules and no `animation:none`.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- A Twine fixture (Harlowe, in `tests/fixtures/twine/`) whose stylesheet hides `tw-story` with
  `opacity:0` + a `forwards` fade-in, and whose passage text sits in a looping `opacity` pulse. Check that the passage
  text is visible (computed `opacity` 1 on `tw-story` and on the text's ancestors, read through the frame script's
  messages or a test hook, since the frame is opaque) and that a link inside can be clicked.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, size budgets, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Colours and contrast in Twine stories ([S1.14](S1.14-twine-readable-colours.md)).
- Timed passages (`(live:)`, `<<timed>>`) are kept: they are delays, not animations.

## Notes & decisions

- **Mechanism:** every element gets `animation-duration:0s`, `animation-delay:0s`, `animation-iteration-count:1`,
  `transition-duration:0s` and `transition-delay:0s` (all `!important`). A zero-length animation still applies its
  `forwards` / `both` fill, so `tw-story.fadeIn` ends at `opacity:1`. `animationend` / `transitionend` now fire at
  once instead of never: Harlowe and SugarCube already wait for them or for timers, and the fixtures and the four
  real Twine stories play as before.
- **Looping animations stop on their last frame.** In Will Not Let Me Go, the pulsing `[` `]` around "what…" end
  at `opacity:0` and are hidden. The link itself stays visible: links, buttons and `tw-link` are forced to
  `opacity:1`. This was chosen over guessing which animations loop.
- **Follow-up (after S1.12):** on the Kindle, Will Not Let Me Go showed only the "yes" / "no" links of a passage
  after a restart, and no brackets around the links: their text sat in animations that end at `opacity:0` (a fade in
  and out, the pulse). The frame script now listens to `animationend` and shows (`opacity:1 !important`, inline) any
  element whose animation ended invisible. Static `opacity:0` without an animation is left alone. The S1.13 e2e
  fixture gets a passage whose text fades in and out for ever from `opacity:0`.
- **Lost on purpose:** Will Not Let Me Go opens with a timed note ("You can leave off reading the story and then
  come back to it… The story will remember where you were") that fades in and out (`fadeInOut`, end state
  `opacity:0`). It is now invisible for those few seconds, then the first passage shows. A message that the author
  makes disappear is not worth a special case.
- **Checked on the PR preview** (Chrome, 636×740): Will Not Let Me Go shows its first passage and "what…" expands
  it; Birdland, The Den and Detritus look as before. The Den's pale dialogue is [S1.14](S1.14-twine-readable-colours.md).
- **E2E:** the Harlowe fixture served with author CSS (story hidden behind a `forwards` fade, links pulsing from
  opacity 0, a 5 s slide). It fails on the old stylesheet (`story` and `link` opacity `0`). No new fixture file:
  the CSS is injected by the test, like the catalogue test's served HTML.
