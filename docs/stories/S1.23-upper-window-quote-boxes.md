## User story

As a player on an e-reader, I want to read the quotation or title card a game shows in its upper window, so that I
don't miss an epigraph or an introduction that is cut to three rows today.

## Background

Found by the [rendering survey of 2026-10-03](../reports/rendering-survey-2026-10-03.md) (S7.3): 7 of 60 games. Inform's
`box` statement draws an epigraph, centred, in the upper window (Curses, Vespers, Jigsaw, An Act of Murder, Hunter in
Darkness); some games draw their title card or introduction there (Filaments, Aisle). The main window is **not**
cleared, so S1.22 does not apply; the game waits for a key (title cards, "[Please press SPACE to begin.]") or goes on
to a line prompt (Vespers: an epigraph after `x me`). The top zone shows the first 3 non-empty rows and "…": the rest
is only in the reader menu (⋯). Example (Curses, intro):

```
              Nine times she waved the fluttering wimple round,
              And made a little plot of magic ground.
              And in that daisied circle, as men say,
              Is Merlin prisoner till the judgement day...

              -- Matthew Arnold, Tristram and Iseult III (1852)
```

**Widened on 2026-10-04 after the [story-file scan](../reports/story-file-scan-2026-10-04.md) (S7.4):** quote boxes
are the most frequent display problem of the catalogue — calls to Inform's box routine in **266 of 2,055 games
(13 %)** — and **426 games** draw in the upper window in their own code (title cards, extra status rows, maps, menus).
Its replays found the same tall windows at a key prompt (Aisle, Filaments) and at a line prompt (Vespers). So the story
covers **any upper window taller than the top zone shows**, outside S1.22's menus, not only `box`. In Glulx, the
Inform library opens a separate buffer window for the box (rock 203): check how the bridge shows it.

## Scope

- **Detect a tall upper window** (a box, a title card, extra rows): more rows than the status line (beyond the game's usual status rows, or more
  than 4 non-empty rows), drawn as a block (rows indented or centred, often framed by blank rows, a quotation and its
  attribution), whatever the input the game waits for. Choose and record the rule from the survey's 7 games; ordinary
  status windows (Bronze's compass, Counterfeit Monkey) and S1.22's menus stay as they are.
- **Show it in the text area**, after the text of the turn (or alone, for a title card while the game waits for a
  key): one centred block, the rows as lines, blank rows kept inside it as spacing, in the game's font. The top zone
  keeps the status line (its first rows) only.
- It stays in the turn's text (Transcript view too), like the text around it, once the game redraws its status line.
- **Lines centred with spaces for a fixed-width screen** (added on 2026-10-04, Anchorhead): games centre a title or a
  prompt with leading spaces for 80 columns (`                   A N C H O R H E A D`,
  `[Press 'R' to restore…]`). As the whitespace is kept (S1.18), such a line wraps badly on a narrow screen. In the
  text area and in the block above: a line whose leading spaces centre it (left and right margins about equal for the
  game's screen width) is shown centred; any other line wider than the screen drops its leading indentation instead of
  wrapping it. Indentation that fits (poems, tables) stays as it is.
- SPEC §3.6 updated.

- [ ] Vespers: the epigraph shown after `x me` reads in full after the turn's text; the status line stays in the top

      for quote boxes and upper-window drawing (`npm run survey:rendering -- --only …`).
- [ ] Checked on the PR preview and on the Kindle, or the Notes say why not.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Detection on the recorded upper windows of the survey's games: boxes vs status rows vs menus.
- The block: centred and indented rows, blank rows inside, the attribution line.
- Space-centred lines: centred vs indented vs too wide (indentation dropped), for 80 and other screen widths.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- The Z-machine and Glulx fixtures' QUOTE (a `box` quotation, added in S7.4), and a quotation at a key prompt (extend
  `tests/fixtures/zmachine/lamp.inf`, rebuilt and committed): it reads in full in the text area, and the status line
  stays.
- A line centred with spaces in the fixture (e.g. its banner or a title line): centred, on one line, at the e-reader
  widths.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, size budgets, CI green.

## Out of scope

- Graphics windows, hyperlinks and multi-column menus (City of Secrets; see the survey).
- Drawing the upper window as a grid.
