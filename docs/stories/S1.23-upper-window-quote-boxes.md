## User story

As a player on an e-reader, I want to read the quotation or title card a game shows in its upper window, so that I
don't miss an epigraph or an introduction that is cut to three rows today.

## Background

Found by the [rendering survey of 2026-10-03](../reports/rendering-survey-2026-10-03.md) (S7.3): 7 of 60 games. Inform's
`box` statement draws an epigraph, centred, in the upper window (Curses, Vespers, Jigsaw, An Act of Murder, Hunter in
Darkness); some games draw their title card or introduction there (Filaments, Aisle). The main window is **not**
cleared, so S1.22 does not apply; the game waits for a key (title cards, "[Please press SPACE to begin.]") or goes on
to a line prompt (Vespers: an epigraph after `x me`). The top zone shows the first 3 non-empty rows and "…": the rest
is only in the reader menu (⋯). Example (Curses, intro):

```
              Nine times she waved the fluttering wimple round,
              And made a little plot of magic ground.
              And in that daisied circle, as men say,
              Is Merlin prisoner till the judgement day...

              -- Matthew Arnold, Tristram and Iseult III (1852)
```

**Widened on 2026-10-04 after the [story-file scan](../reports/story-file-scan-2026-10-04.md) (S7.4):** quote boxes
are the most frequent display problem of the catalogue — calls to Inform's box routine in **266 of 2,055 games
(13 %)** — and **426 games** draw in the upper window in their own code (title cards, extra status rows, maps, menus).
Its replays found the same tall windows at a key prompt (Aisle, Filaments) and at a line prompt (Vespers). So the story
covers **any upper window taller than the top zone shows**, outside S1.22's menus, not only `box`. In Glulx, the
Inform library opens a separate buffer window for the box (rock 203): check how the bridge shows it.

## Scope

- **Detect a tall upper window** (a box, a title card, extra rows): more rows than the status line (beyond the game's usual status rows, or more
  than 4 non-empty rows), drawn as a block (rows indented or centred, often framed by blank rows, a quotation and its
  attribution), whatever the input the game waits for. Choose and record the rule from the survey's 7 games; ordinary
  status windows (Bronze's compass, Counterfeit Monkey) and S1.22's menus stay as they are.
- **Show it in the text area**, after the text of the turn (or alone, for a title card while the game waits for a
  key): one centred block, the rows as lines, blank rows kept inside it as spacing, in the game's font. The top zone
  keeps the status line (its first rows) only.
- It stays in the turn's text (Transcript view too), like the text around it, once the game redraws its status line.
- **Lines centred with spaces for a fixed-width screen** (added on 2026-10-04, Anchorhead): games centre a title or a
  prompt with leading spaces for 80 columns (`                   A N C H O R H E A D`,
  `[Press 'R' to restore…]`). As the whitespace is kept (S1.18), such a line wraps badly on a narrow screen. In the
  text area and in the block above: a line whose leading spaces centre it (left and right margins about equal for the
  game's screen width) is shown centred; any other line wider than the screen drops its leading indentation instead of
  wrapping it. Indentation that fits (poems, tables) stays as it is.
- SPEC §3.6 updated.

## Out of scope

- Graphics windows, hyperlinks and multi-column menus (City of Secrets; see the survey).
- Drawing the upper window as a grid.

## Notes & decisions

- **Detection rule** (`splitUpper`, `src/engines/transcript.ts`), chosen from the survey's games: the status line is
  the rows above the first blank row (none for an opening epigraph, whose window starts blank); the rows after it are
  a box when at least one has text and all those that do are indented by 2 spaces or more (Inform centres its box; a
  map or a list of exits starts at the margin). No box under a status line taller than 4 rows (the top zone shows
  those as before). S1.22's menus come first: no box while the main window is cleared and still empty.
- **Rows drawn again after a command are status rows, not a box.** Metamorphoses draws its four humours, centred,
  under its location on every turn: the first turn shows them as a box, the next one moves them back to the top zone,
  for good (`fixedBox`). An Inform box is gone at the next turn.
- **Place: at the start of the turn**, after the echoed command (or opening the screen, for an epigraph drawn before
  the game clears its window), not after the turn's text as first written: the window sits above the turn's text, and
  an epigraph reads before the text it heads ("Curses are like young chickens…" before "Welcome to CURSES / [Please
  press SPACE to begin.]"). The criterion for Vespers was reworded. The box is shown there while the game shows it
  (`withBox`), and kept as paragraphs there when the upper window is redrawn without it, so nothing moves.
- **Layout:** one paragraph per group of rows (the blank rows between groups become paragraph spacing), one line per
  row, every line centred, in the reading font; the spaces that placed the rows are dropped. Rows are not re-flowed
  into paragraphs (Aisle's introduction keeps its rows): telling a wrapped paragraph from a poem was not reliable.
- **Glulx:** the library's quote box is a buffer window of its own (rock 203), whose text the bridge mixed into the
  main text before, with a clear that started a new screen. The bridge now keeps the main buffer window (the one that
  asks for input, else the first opened) and reports the text of any other one as a `quote` block, shown as a box; its
  closing ends it. The same applies to a game's side panel (only 2 games of the catalogue open several buffer windows,
  S7.4).
- **Saves** keep which paragraphs are box rows (`boxes`, like `screens`), so a restored box stays centred; older saves
  have none.
- **Lines laid out for 80 columns** (added after Anchorhead's title): only lines with 8 leading spaces or more, so
  indented paragraphs and poems stay as typed (S1.18). Centred when the left and right margins on 80 columns differ by
  4 or less (Anchorhead's title: 29 / 32; "[Press 'R'…]": 15 / 19); indent dropped when the line is wider than 80
  (Filaments' byline after 88 spaces); otherwise the indent becomes a share of the column (`text-indent` in %:
  Filaments' "F.I.L.A.M.E.N.T.S", 25 spaces → 31.3 %), which never wraps a run of spaces on a narrow screen.
- **Survey** (`npm run survey:rendering`, [report of 2026-10-05](../reports/rendering-survey-2026-10-05.md)): new
  findings "Box drawn in the upper window" (informative) and "Boxes on many turns" (3 or more kept: a check for status
  rows read as a box). Rerun on 47 games: the 8 of the story, Bronze, Counterfeit Monkey, City of Secrets, Lost Pig,
  Metamorphoses, and the most-rated games the S7.4 scan lists for quote boxes, upper-window drawing and extra buffer
  windows: no "tall upper window" left but City of Secrets' HELP (hyperlinks, out of scope); boxes in 23 games, the
  menus unchanged (17 games); "many boxes" in Tapestry only, whose boxes are
  real epigraphs (one per chapter). Shrapnel flashes its intro one fragment per key in the upper window: the fragments
  now read one after the other in the text.
- **Fixture:** `lamp.inf` (Z-machine) gained VERSE (a box at a key prompt) and a title centred with 30 spaces in the
  intro; the S7.4 scan test now counts its 2 quote boxes.
- **Checked** by the owner on the PR #81 preview and on the Kindle (2026-10-09): the fixtures' QUOTE and VERSE, and
  real games (Anchorhead's opening screen among them).
