## User story

As a player on an e-reader, I want to answer the parser's "What do you want to …?" with a tap on an object, so that
a chip that can be used alone or completed (`LOOK`, `TALK TO`, `ASK`) works in two taps without typing.

## Background

Raised by the owner on 2026-10-03, during S1.19: some commands work alone or with an object (`LOOK` / `LOOK AT X`,
`TALK TO` / `TALK TO X`). A long press was considered and set aside: on the Kindle's browser it may start a text
selection or the dictionary (not measured), it is hard to discover, and its timing is unreliable on e-ink. A chip
that always waits for Enter was also set aside: it doubles the taps of the most frequent commands (directions,
Look, Inventory).

The parser already handles an incomplete command: after `talk to` or `look at`, Inform asks "Whom do you want to
talk to?" / "What do you want to look at?" (French library: "À qui voulez-vous parler ?", "Que voulez-vous
regarder ?"), and a noun alone at the next prompt completes the command. When it cannot tell two objects apart it
asks "Which do you mean, the brass lamp or the oil lamp?" and also takes the answer alone. Today, at these prompts
the verbs row is shown, and the player must tap a word in the text and then Enter.

So a tap keeps sending at once, and the game's question is what asks for the object.

## Scope

- While the game waits for a line, when the last paragraph before the prompt is the parser asking for an object:
  - "What / Whom do you want to … ?" (EN), "Que / Qui / À qui / Sur quoi … voulez-vous … ?" (FR), the forms of the
    Inform 6 and Inform 7 libraries in the game language (record the patterns and their source);
  - "Which do you mean, the X, the Y or the Z?" / "Précisez : le X ou le Y ?": the options named in the question.
- The verbs row then shows **noun chips**, as after a verb ending with "…": the options named in a "Which do you
  mean" question first (in their order), otherwise the recently mentioned objects (`recentNouns`); ✕ goes back to the
  verbs. A tap sends the **noun alone** (the parser completes the command).
- Answer chips (S1.17) and named-command chips (S1.19) are unchanged; a parser question wins over them on that turn.
- SPEC §3.6 (Command input) updated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In the Z-machine fixture, `talk to` (or `examine`) without an object, then a tap on a noun chip: the game
      answers for that object.
- [ ] A "Which do you mean" question gets one chip per option, in order; a tap answers it.
- [ ] Ordinary turns keep the verbs row (no noun row without a parser question).
- [ ] French: the French library's questions are recognised (unit test on recorded text).
- [ ] Checked on the PR preview and on the Kindle, or the Notes say why not.

## Required tests

**Unit (Vitest)**

- Recognition: the EN and FR Inform 6 / Inform 7 forms (what / whom, "Which do you mean", "Précisez"), the options of
  a "Which do you mean" question (2, 3, with "or" / "ou", with articles); no match for a question to the player
  ("Do you want to…?", S1.17), nor for a question inside the story's prose that is not the last paragraph.

**E2E (Playwright)**

- Z-machine fixture (`lamp.inf`, extended if needed with two objects that share a word): an incomplete command, a
  tap on a noun chip, the game's answer; a "Which do you mean" question answered with a chip.

Plus the global gates: lint, `es-check es5` on the legacy bundle, size budgets, CI green.

## Out of scope

- A long press or any new gesture on chips.
- Changing what a tap on a verb or named chip does.
- Questions from games not built with Inform (TADS, Adrift…): only if their form is the same.
